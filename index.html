            About HBU Arcade & privacy

            Games are provided by their original publishers and may load third-party content. HbuOS does not assign age ratings; check the publisher's information and supervise younger players as appropriate.

            Favorites, recent games, and basic usage counts are saved only in this browser on this device. HbuOS does not send analytics to a tracking service. Publishers may collect data when you play their games; review their privacy information. The home screen also loads an advertisement from Adsterra, which may collect information through its ad services; see Adsterra's privacy policy.

            Accounts, global chat messages, and DMs are stored in Supabase. Passwords are hashed, but DMs are not end-to-end encrypted, so avoid sharing private information. Every message is checked by an AI moderator (mod-bot, using OpenAI): curse words are removed, hateful messages can lead to a chat ban of up to a week, and you can ask it questions with @mod-bot in the main chat. Your student ID is stored with your account but never shown. Usernames and main-chat messages are visible to all visitors.
